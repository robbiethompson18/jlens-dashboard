What am I looking at?

J-lens (Anthropic, 2026) maps the residual stream at layer ℓ through the average Jacobian to the final layer, then unembeds: softmax(WU·norm(Jℓhℓ)). It reads words the activation is poised to make the model say, now or later. It is not a next-token predictor.

Logit lens unembeds the residual directly. J++ (Ayonrinde & Lindsey, 2026) filters the Jacobian; R-lens builds it from a RelP backward pass for cleaner early layers.

Layers are shown as % depth. The paper finds the J-lens useful from about 38% to 92%; the last row (100%) is the model's own next-token prediction and is identical across lenses.

Each cell keeps the top 20 word-like tokens (letters and digits only, as in the reference viewer and Neuronpedia), with full-vocabulary probability. A word longer than one token shows only as its first piece.

Words in the workspace counts, over the selected tokens (drag to select) and layers, how often each word sits in the top N. "Unsaid only" hides words that appear anywhere in the transcript text.
