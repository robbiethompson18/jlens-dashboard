What am I looking at?

J-lens (Anthropic, 2026) maps the residual stream at layer ℓ through the average Jacobian to the final layer, then unembeds: softmax(WU·norm(Jℓhℓ)). It reads words the activation is poised to make the model say, now or later. It is not a next-token predictor.

Logit lens unembeds the residual directly. J++ (Ayonrinde & Lindsey, 2026) filters the Jacobian; R-lens builds it from a RelP backward pass for cleaner early layers.

Layers are shown as % depth. The paper finds the J-lens useful from about 38% to 92%; the last row (100%) is the model's own next-token prediction and is identical across lenses.

Each cell keeps the top 20 word-like tokens (letters and digits only, as in the reference viewer and Neuronpedia), with full-vocabulary probability. A word longer than one token shows only as its first piece.

Words in the workspace counts, over the selected tokens (drag to select) and layers, how often each word sits in the top N. "Unsaid only" hides words that appear anywhere in the transcript text.

Oracle lens and NLA are prose readouts: the activation is injected into a prompt and a reader model writes what it holds. See "Where the O-lens and NLA came from". On the WorkspaceBench items both come from the authors' published runs; there the J-lens is their top-10 bag without scores, shaded by rank. A search matches the prose as whole words.

Where the O-lens and NLA came from

O-lens is Robbie's own oracle lens for Qwen3.6-27B (robbiethompson2018/oracle-lens-qwen3.6-27b-pilot, repo oracle-lens-reconstruction): a replication of the Anthropic workspace-paper appendix recipe. A reconstructor maps a short phrase to the whitened residual direction the model holds before saying it; the oracle, a LoRA on Qwen itself, is fed the activation (its unit vector × 16000 replaces one token's input embedding) and emits phrases whose reconstructor directions explain the activation. Warm-started by distilling an NN-OMP teacher, then 40 REINFORCE steps on variance explained. Pilot scale, one H100, held-out FVE 0.236 at 8 tokens × 10 phrases (ceselder's full-scale July run: 0.21–0.22). Read here at layer 42, its training layer, as 8 phrases of 4 tokens, greedy decoding, at every token.

NLA is ceselder's natural language autoencoder for Qwen3.6-27B (ceselder/qwen3.6-27b-nla-rl, step 400, trained with easyNLA after the Anthropic 2026 NLA paper): a separate verbalizer copy of Qwen that is handed the activation (norm-matched, added at its marker token) and writes a free-form explanation; it was trained with GRPO against a reconstructor to maximize variance explained, converging near 0.77 FVE. It only reads layer 42, the layer it was trained on, so both prose lenses sit at the same layer. Sampled once per token at temperature 1, top-p 0.95, top-k 64, up to 256 tokens.

Neither lens sees the other tokens: each readout is from one residual vector at one position, so what it names is what that single activation carries.
